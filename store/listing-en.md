**Full description:**
SabaiBiz is the back-office assistant for cafés, restaurants and shops in Thailand.

- Photograph a supplier invoice — AI reads products, quantities and prices for you
- See every price change over time and get alerted when a supplier raises prices
- Build recipes and see the real profit margin per dish
- Track your team: shifts, absences and payroll
- Works in English and Thai

Free plan available. Employees are never charged as seats.
